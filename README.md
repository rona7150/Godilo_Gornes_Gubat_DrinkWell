# Godilo_Gornes_Gubat_DrinkWell
DrinkWell - Smart water reminder &amp; hydration tracker. Never forget to drink water again.

About the App: 

DrinkWell is a hydration tracking app designed to help you build healthy drinking habits. Register, set your daily water goal, and let the app handle the reminders. Track every sip and watch your progress improve over time.

Features:
- User Registration: Create your account and personalize your hydration goals
- Water Intake Tracker: Log every glass/cups with just one tap  
- Smart Reminders: Get timely notifications so you never miss a drink
- Display Progress: See your daily/weekly intake at a glance with clean UI
- Analytics: View detailed stats and trends to understand your hydration habits
